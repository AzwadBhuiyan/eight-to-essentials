# Eight to Essentials

_Check your Essential Eight. Plan your move to ASD Essentials._

Built by [Azwad Bhuiyan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/azwad-bhuiyan).

> **Status:** in development. A full README, live demo link and screenshots will be added before the v1.0.0 release.

Eight to Essentials is a free, open-source, browser-only self-assessment and planning aid. It is not an official ASD assessment, audit or certification, and it is not legal, compliance or insurance advice. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Australian Signals Directorate (ASD) or the Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC).
